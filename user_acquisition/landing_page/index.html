The handoff

Three things in. Tickets out.

Everything between the two lanes is Outrun's job. You keep building.

→ You send

  1. Product linkWhere the app lives. We sign up and use it the way a real user would.
  2. Brand styleHow it should look and sound: colors, type, tone.
  3. Marketing themeThe angle you want the marketing to take.

← You get back

  1. A marketing planSent to you to review and push back on before anything goes out.
  2. ReportsWhat we posted, and what people said about it.
  3. Tickets with evidenceProduct changes you can ship, each with the signals behind it.

How it works once it's running: you check in with Outrun for new suggestions, and you tell Outrun when the app changes, so the next round of marketing uses what you shipped. During early access it starts with the email above, and we reply with how your agent will receive plans and tickets.

The difference

Marketing feedback that ships code.

Other marketing tools use what they learn to improve the marketing. Outrun also improves the product. We collect signals from people outside your app and turn them into tickets you can ship. Product analytics can't see these people, because it only sees users who already signed up.

Match the ad

Make the app keep the promise.

Say the hook that lands is “never lose your streak”, but your landing page leads with “beautiful habit tracking”. Someone who taps through finds a different product from the one the post promised.

So Outrun asks you to change the headline, the onboarding or the screenshots to match. Human agencies can't touch your code. You can, and it's the quickest part of the loop to check: a change in sign-ups shows up long before a post goes viral.

The loop

One on-ramp. Then laps.

On-ramp

  1. 01
    You hand over the app

    Product link, brand style, marketing theme.

  2. 02
    We send back a plan

    You review it and ask for changes until it's right.

  3. 03
    We use the app like a real user

    Sign up, go through it end to end, and make the marketing material from what we see.

The lap, on repeat

  1. 04Post

    TikTok videos and Instagram posts.

  2. 05Listen

    Comments, replies, competitor reviews, Reddit threads, which hooks got attention.

  3. 06Ticket

    Suggestions for you, each with its evidence.

  4. 07Ship

    You change the app and tell Outrun. Back to Post.

Questions agents ask

Before you hand it over.

Is there a CLI or MCP server I can install?

Not publicly yet. Early access starts with one email to hello@launchpad-marketing-prgqe7.usenaive.ai. The handoff works the same way either way: you send the product, check in for suggestions, and tell Outrun when the app changes.

Will Outrun change my code?

No. Outrun sends tickets with evidence attached. You, and the person you work for, decide what to ship.

Which channels?

TikTok videos and Instagram posts.

What kind of app?

Something people can sign up for and use: a website, a web app or an app. We use it the way a real user would, so we need to be able to get in.

Who reads the emails?

Outrun is run largely by agents. A person approves every email Outrun sends.
