Our naive autonomous company would be a go-to-market/marketing agency that's optimized for other agents.

For example, if someone were to be using agents to build, let's say, a habit tracker app that they wanted to market, they would simply be able to tell Claude Code or Codex (whatever agent they're using) to use our marketing agency. We would have a connector, let's say a CLI or MCP server, to allow their agent to send all the necessary materials over to our marketing agency. Our marketing agency would take care of the rest of the marketing process for them, including managing their social media, let's say TikTok and Instagram, and also it could be reaching out to creators or managing a newsletter, etc.

Another reason this would be good is that, in the demo, we can create our own customer. To demo our agency, we can simply spawn another Claude Code session that generates another app (I'd say some sort of a website, web app, or app). Then we can demo that agent simply sending everything to our autonomous agency, and then we would take care of the rest of the marketing flow for them.

Other notes on this idea:
Agents will be the primary way for other people to interact with other agencies. For example, people don't want to go through the back-and-forth of communicating with a marketing agency themselves. People want to have their own agent do everything.


1. A mechanism nobody else has: the two-way loop with the builder’s agent
Most marketing-agency teams will be one-way: take materials, post content. Yours reports market signals back to the customer’s coding agent in structured form, and that agent changes the product. The pitch becomes “we’re the first agency where marketing feedback ships code,” which is a different thing from “AI posts on social media.”

Focus: we’re building an autonomous marketing agency company that serves other autonomous companies

1. We tell your coding agent what to build, not just what to post.
 Every other marketing tool’s feedback loop improves the marketing. Ours also improves the product. We collect signals from people outside the app: comments on our posts, complaints in competitors’ reviews, Reddit threads, and which hooks got attention. We turn them into tickets the coding agent can ship, each with evidence attached. Product analytics tools can’t see any of this, because they only see users who already signed up.
2. We make the product match what the ad promised.
 If our best-performing hook is “never lose your streak” but the landing page leads with “beautiful habit tracking,” we ask the agent to change the headline, onboarding or screenshots to match. Human agencies can’t touch code. Other AI marketing tools only open PRs for tracking setup. This is also the fastest loop to prove, because a signup-rate change shows up long before a post goes viral.

What the actual workflow should look like:
The originating agent sends a link to the product, along with what they want the brand style, and marketing theme to be
We then create a marketing plan (and we send it back to the originating agent to review and iterate on?)
The study agent would triage the app / website with a browser / simulator, sign up as a real user, then crawl the entire app and generate marketing materials for it
It would then start the marketing loop, generating and distributing marketing materials across instagram, tiktok, etc.
It would monitor the comments and responses from those channels, and return feedback to the originating agent
The originating agent would receive the suggestions back through polling with our CLI. it would receive instruction to setup the recurring automation.
It can then return further suggestions or notify our agency when / if there’s a major update to the app
